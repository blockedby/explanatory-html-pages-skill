Expense review: scope and outcome

The process starts when an employee submits an expense. A reviewer checks the receipt and records a decision. Payment execution is outside this small process; the result is a recorded review, not a promise that money has moved.

Actor
Expense reviewer.
Input
A submitted expense with supporting evidence.
Output
A recorded decision available to the next process.

The process in BPMN

Make exceptions explicit

Missing evidence and disputed decisions need their own agreed handling. Add those branches when the rules are available; do not silently interpret a missing receipt as an approval.
