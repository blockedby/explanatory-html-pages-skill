Reserve two units during checkout

Trigger
The customer proceeds from basket to payment.
Preconditions
The checkout service is authenticated; item SKU-42 exists; the requested quantity is two.
Success outcome
One active hold owns two units until confirmation, cancellation, or its ten-minute deadline.
Failure outcome
No partial hold is created. The checkout keeps the basket and explains the rejection.

Main scenario

  1. Checkout submits SKU-42, quantity 2, and a new idempotency key.
  2. Inventory validates the request and checks stock inside the same transaction that creates the hold.
  3. Inventory commits the hold and an outbox record for its creation event.
  4. Inventory returns the reservation ID and expiry time. Checkout shows the payment step.

Alternative paths

A1 · Step 2: insufficient stock
Inventory returns 409 insufficient_stock without creating a hold. Checkout asks the customer to reduce the quantity; the scenario ends.
A2 · Step 4: response lost
Checkout repeats the identical request with the same key. Inventory returns the original result, not a second hold; resume at step 4.
A3 · Before payment: hold expires
Checkout requests a new hold before proceeding. If stock is no longer available, follow A1; the expired hold cannot be confirmed.
