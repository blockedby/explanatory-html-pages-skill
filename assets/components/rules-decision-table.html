When may a hold be confirmed?

In this example, confirmation converts an active hold into a committed allocation. Inventory evaluates the rules in the listed order using its own clock and a locked reservation record.

First matching row wins; a dash means the condition is irrelevant
RuleCaller owns hold?StateNow before expiry?Result
BR-01NoAny—Reject without exposing the hold's state.
BR-02YesConfirmed—Return the existing confirmation; no new allocation.
BR-03YesCancelled or expired—Reject; a new reservation is required.
BR-04YesActiveNoExpire and release the hold; reject confirmation.
BR-05YesActiveYesConfirm once and record the allocation.

Boundary: at exactly expires_at, an active hold is expired. An already confirmed hold remains confirmed after that timestamp. Authentication and malformed requests are handled before this table.
